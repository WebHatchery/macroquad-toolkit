# Concept Art: Archive Expedition

This is a non-runtime concept-art set for the toolkit's neutral reference world:
a teal-and-amber science-fiction archive explored by an archivist and her small
service drone.

## Direction

- Quiet, reverent exploration rather than combat-first science fiction.
- Near-black navy, oxidized teal, cyan glass, aged brass, and warm practical lights.
- Graphic-novel realism with readable silhouettes and buildable hard-surface spaces.
- UI-safe compositions with dark negative space where title or menu copy can sit.

## Images

- `archivist_vault_key_art.png` — refined 16:9 title-screen mood piece.
- `archive_chamber_environment.png` — 16:9 environment and level-layout reference.
- `archivist_drone_design_sheet.png` — protagonist clothing, face, and drone design sheet.

## Provenance

Generated with the built-in Codex image-generation tool on 2026-08-25 from
original prompts shaped around the existing archivist portrait and drone
reference assets in `assets/images/`. The final key art is an iteration of the
first key-art pass with a simpler hologram and quieter negative space.
